# Co-Designing Better Multi-Hazard Early Warning Systems with Community Feedback

<img src="groupanalyzing.jpg" alt="groupanalyzing.jpg" style="width:500px;">

Reliable early warning systems begin with an understanding of their uses -- the risks they face in context, their informational needs, and the actions they are able to take.

The workshop brings together key stakeholders to review hazard analysis results, discuss risk communication and alert protocols, and gather sector-specific feedback to strengthen multi-hazard early warning systems in Belize.

This week, our research group at Columbia has been working with the NMS to develop appropriate tools for multi-hazard early warning in Belize. As the users of these tools, your input is crucial in this process.

Today, we will start by working through a scenario exercise that illustrates the key choices decision-makers face, and how a co-design process can address them. 

Then, the NMS will present the results of the hazard mapping and early warning threshold analysis that we have co-developed thus far, which incorporates the input you have provided in advance of this workshop. 

After lunch, we will go deeper on your feedback, discussing how these early warning systems might be improved, the key benchmarks they should reach, and the actions you might plan on the basis of their alerts. Your feedback will be captured and incorporated into our final analysis and recommendations report for NMS and WFP Belize.
